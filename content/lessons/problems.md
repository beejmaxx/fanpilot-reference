# Find a problem, then find its idea.

This library lists every numbered LeetCode problem in a frozen public catalog, with its public topic tags. Use it to choose practice and to see which problems this guide actually discusses. A topic tag tells you what a solution may use; it does not explain why that technique follows from the problem.

## How to use the list

Pick a topic you have studied, filter to problems this guide discusses, and attempt one without reading its tags. Afterward, compare your reasoning with the linked chapter or worked lesson. To test transfer, choose an untaught problem from the same topic and write the baseline, bottleneck, and enabling observation before coding.

The links in the last column point to pattern chapters that cite a problem and worked lessons that solve one in full. Most problems have no lesson yet. The list makes those gaps visible; it is not a claim of coverage.

## Where the data comes from

Numbers, titles, URLs, categories, and topic tags come from a snapshot of the [doocs/leetcode](https://github.com/doocs/leetcode) problem index, which mirrors LeetCode's public metadata. Problem statements belong to LeetCode and are linked rather than copied. Company frequency lists are deliberately omitted: they are derived from gated data and go stale quickly.
